\section{Purpose}
In recent years, the use of bicycles has significantly increased, both as a means of daily transportation and as part of urban sustainability initiatives.
However, despite this growing interest, it remains difficult for cyclists to access reliable and up-to-date information about existing bike paths, their conditions, and potential obstacles along the way.
Such a lack of information often makes it challenging to plan safe, efficient, and enjoyable routes, discouraging many users from adopting cycling as a regular mode of transport.

\textbf{Best Bike Paths (BBP)} is designed to overcome these limitations by providing a collaborative and intelligent platform that collects, analyzes, and shares data about cycling routes.
Through the combination of user-generated content and automated data collection from mobile sensors, BBP enables users to record their trips, monitor their performance, and contribute to a shared, continuously updated map of bike paths.

The system also integrates external weather information to enhance the understanding of riding conditions, and it supports recommendation features to suggest the best paths between two points based on quality, safety, and user preferences. %non mi pare che ci siano preferenze espresse dai ciclisti

\section{Scope}
\label{subsec:scope}%

Best Bike Paths (BBP) is a platform that supports cyclists in creating and consulting a collaborative inventory of bike paths. The platform manages the path status through standardized categories (optimal, medium, sufficient, requires maintenance) and tracks the presence of relevant obstacles such as potholes or other issues that may compromise safety. % bisognerebbe considerare secondo me che un percorso precedentemenyte esistente potrebbe essere bloccato o non esistere più

BBP provides the following core functionalities:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Support cyclists in recording and storing their trips with performance statistics.
    \item Enable manual and automatic insertion of bike path information collected from mobile devices.
    \item Allow users (registered and unregistered) to explore bike paths on a map between chosen origin and destination points.
    \item Aggregate and reconcile path data from multiple users, considering temporal proximity and consensus.
    \item Enrich trip data with meteorological information obtained from external services.
\end{itemize}

\section{Definitions, Acronyms, Abbreviations}
\label{sec:definition_acronyms_abbreviations}

\subsection{Definitions} \begin{description} \item[BBP (Best Bike Paths)] The collaborative platform, subject of this document, designed to collect, analyze, and share data about cycling routes. \item[Registered User] A user who has created a profile on the platform and can record trips, insert data, and manage their profile. \item[Unregistered User] A user who can only consult information and view maps of available bike paths. \item[Path Status] A standardized rating of a path's condition (e.g., optimal, medium, sufficient, requires maintenance). \item[Trip] The recording of a single bicycle journey, which includes GPS data, sensor readings, and statistics. 
\item[StreetSegment] The fundamental unit of the system, representing a stretch of road between two intersections or junctions. Each StreetSegment contains no intersections within it and constitutes the most atomic level of the road network.

\item[Path / BikePath] A sequence of connected StreetSegments selected to ensure safe and comfortable conditions for cyclists. A Path is considered suitable when the motor vehicle speed limit is reasonable for cycling use or when a dedicated bike lane is available.

\end{description}
